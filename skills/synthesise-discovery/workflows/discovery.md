# Discovery workflow

1. Clarify creator's intended outcome and constraints.
2. Inventory capabilities, experience, specialist knowledge and methods.
3. Capture repeatable outcomes and supporting proof/artifacts.
4. Identify preferred audiences, problems and delivery formats.
5. Record capacity, resources, distribution and boundaries.
6. Separate demonstrated strengths from aspirations.
7. Summarize the profile and ask the single most important remaining question.

Output: expertise inventory, credibility map, audience hypotheses, constraints and evidence gaps. Use the adjacent expertise inventory template.
