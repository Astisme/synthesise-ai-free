# Segmentation and jobs-to-be-done reference

## Segment on meaningful differences

Useful segment dimensions include triggering situation, task, behaviour, desired progress, constraints, sophistication, current workaround, buying authority and access. Use demographics only when they materially affect the job, buying context or delivery.

## Job map

For each segment capture:
- Trigger and context: what happened that makes the job active?
- Functional job: what is the person trying to accomplish?
- Emotional/social job: how do they want to feel or be perceived?
- Current workaround and alternatives, including doing nothing.
- Friction, switching costs and failed attempts.
- Desired outcome and observable success criteria.
- Frequency, severity, urgency and consequences.
- Buyer, budget owner, approval process and objections.
- Where and how the segment can be reached.

## Evidence discipline

Keep direct quotes verbatim only when supplied or sourced, with provenance and permission where needed. A small convenience sample can reveal language and mechanisms but cannot establish prevalence. Label inferred personas and untested buying assumptions.
