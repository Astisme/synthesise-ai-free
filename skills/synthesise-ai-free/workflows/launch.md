# Launch workflow

1. Confirm offer, scope, pricing hypothesis and delivery capacity.
2. Define launch audience, channel and message hypotheses.
3. Prepare landing page, checkout, onboarding and support process.
4. Create a dated campaign calendar with owner and acceptance criteria.
5. QA the end-to-end customer journey.
6. Launch with transparent claims and beta/pre-sale terms.
7. Track qualified leads, conversion, revenue, refunds, activation, completion and outcomes.
8. Interview customers, identify friction and prioritize iteration.
9. Update the project brief and decide the next operating cycle.

Use templates/launch-plan.md. Metrics need a definition, source and review cadence.
