# Market intelligence workflow

1. Define the decision, segment, geography and research questions.
2. Identify direct competitors, indirect alternatives, DIY and status quo.
3. Collect primary sources for positioning, features, prices and proof.
4. Collect customer problem language and counterexamples.
5. Compare alternatives using a consistent matrix.
6. Identify underserved jobs and positioning hypotheses.
7. Cite sources, dates and confidence; state research limitations.
8. Translate findings into product and validation decisions.

Do not report competitor revenue, market share or demand without reliable evidence. Use references/research-protocol.md.
