# Product building workflow

1. Define before/after customer states and a bounded promise.
2. Select a format suited to the job and delivery economics.
3. Design the transformation mechanism and milestones.
4. Map outcomes to modules, activities and deliverables.
5. Build curriculum backward from evidence of learning.
6. Create workbook prompts, worked examples and templates.
7. Define onboarding, support, accessibility and completion criteria.
8. Review scope, quality, feasibility and dependencies.
9. Produce a charter and prioritized production backlog.

Use templates/product-charter.md, templates/curriculum.md and templates/workbook-outline.md.
