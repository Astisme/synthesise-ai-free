# Quality gates

## Opportunity
- Is the audience specific and reachable?
- Is the problem evidenced or labelled as a hypothesis?
- Is creator expertise credible and supported?
- Is differentiation meaningful to the buyer?
- Are unknowns and score confidence visible?

## Validation
- Was the experiment run, not merely proposed?
- Are sample, recruitment, timeframe and limitations recorded?
- Was behavioural or payment evidence collected?
- Were thresholds defined in advance?
- Does the conclusion match evidence strength?

## Product
- Is transformation specific and realistic?
- Does each module map to an outcome?
- Are exercises, deliverables and completion criteria concrete?
- Are prerequisites, exclusions and support boundaries explicit?
- Is production feasible?

## Launch
- Is the offer understandable and honest?
- Are checkout, onboarding and support ready?
- Are owners, dependencies, dates and acceptance criteria assigned?
- Are metrics defined with source and cadence?
- Is there a post-launch learning loop?
