# Commercial strategy

## Positioning

For [audience] who [situation/problem], [product] helps achieve [outcome] through [mechanism]. Unlike [credible alternative], it [relevant difference]. Support differences with evidence or label them hypotheses.

## Offer architecture

Specify buyer/user, problem/outcome, format/scope/delivery schedule, assets/support, prerequisites/exclusions, price/currency/tax, payment schedule, refund/cancellation terms for legal review, risks and proof.

## Tier design

Use tiers only when they serve materially different needs (self-serve, guided, implementation). State differences in access, support, customization and delivery. Avoid fake anchors and arbitrary feature stuffing.

## Pricing hypotheses

Triangulate comparable alternatives, problem cost, buyer budget, delivery cost, support time, capacity, positioning and risk. State currency, billing basis and assumptions. Test price through real offers where appropriate; surveys alone are weak evidence.

## Basic economics

Net revenue = gross receipts − discounts − refunds − taxes collected for authorities.

Contribution per sale = net revenue − variable delivery/payment/acquisition costs.

Break-even units = fixed launch costs / contribution per sale.

State excluded costs. Do not invent conversion rates, CAC, LTV or market size. For recurring offers, model churn, servicing cost and continuing value.

## Ethical selling

No fabricated proof, guaranteed results, misleading scarcity, hidden fees or unsupported income claims. Be transparent about beta status, delivery dates, limitations and refund terms.
