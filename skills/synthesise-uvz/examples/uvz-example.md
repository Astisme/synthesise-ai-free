# Illustrative UVZ example (fictional)

This example demonstrates the reasoning format only. It is not customer research or evidence of demand.

## Creator capability
A freelance designer has repeatable experience creating brand systems for small service businesses, with a portfolio of completed projects.

## Audience hypothesis
Early-stage local service businesses that need consistent sales and marketing materials but cannot justify a full agency engagement.

## Problem hypothesis
Owners spend time producing inconsistent assets and struggle to communicate a coherent brand.

## Opportunity hypothesis
A guided brand-system toolkit that helps owners define a usable identity and apply it consistently across essential touchpoints.

## Provisional comparison

| Dimension | Rating | Evidence / limitation |
|---|---:|---|
| Expertise and proof | 4 | Portfolio exists; outcome evidence not yet audited |
| Problem intensity | 3 | Plausible pain; requires interviews |
| Willingness to pay | 1 | No purchase evidence |
| Reachability | 3 | Local business communities are possible channels |
| Differentiation | 3 | Toolkit format is common; mechanism needs sharper definition |
| Delivery feasibility | 4 | Creator has relevant skills |
| Strategic fit | 4 | Assumed fit; confirm creator preference |

Scores are provisional. Do not treat the total as validated demand.

## Cheapest useful test
Interview a small, deliberately recruited group of target owners about recent branding tasks, workarounds, costs and buying decisions. Then offer a clearly scoped paid pilot. Define decision thresholds before recruiting.

## Decision
Research further; do not build the full toolkit until problem and payment assumptions have stronger evidence.
