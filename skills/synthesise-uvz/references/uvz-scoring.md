# UVZ opportunity scoring

This rubric compares opportunity hypotheses. It is a prioritization aid, not a forecast or validated instrument.

| Dimension | Weight | 1 — weak | 3 — moderate | 5 — strong |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Expertise and proof | 20% | Little relevant capability or proof | Relevant experience, partial proof | Deep, current, repeatable expertise with credible evidence |
| Problem intensity | 20% | Infrequent or low consequence | Recurring inconvenience | Urgent, costly or consequential problem |
| Willingness-to-pay evidence | 15% | No evidence beyond compliments | Comparable spend or qualified intent | Actual paid commitment or existing spend |
| Audience reachability | 10% | Hard to identify or access | Several possible channels | Concentrated, reachable segment |
| Differentiation | 15% | Commodity offer | Some distinct approach | Relevant mechanism and clear alternatives gap |
| Delivery feasibility | 10% | High uncertainty or unsupported promise | Feasible with constraints | Repeatable, scoped and supportable |
| Strategic fit | 10% | Poor fit with goals/capacity | Acceptable fit | Strong energy, economics and long-term fit |

## Calculation

For each dimension record rating (1–5), evidence, confidence (high/medium/low) and unknowns.

Weighted score out of 100 = sum(weight × rating / 5). A rating of 4 on a 20%-weighted dimension contributes 16 points. Do not treat missing evidence as confirmed; flag provisional ratings and low confidence.

## Interpretation

- 80–100: high-priority hypothesis; still validate demand and delivery.
- 65–79: promising; address largest evidence gaps.
- 50–64: exploratory; test a narrower segment or sharper problem.
- Below 50: deprioritize unless a strategic reason justifies a small test.

Bands are heuristic. Compare options using the same weights. If top scores are within five points or low-confidence dimensions drive the result, run sensitivity analysis and choose the cheapest discriminating experiment.

## Required output

| Opportunity | Expertise | Problem | WTP | Reach | Difference | Feasibility | Fit | Total | Confidence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

Follow with rationale, unknowns, risks and a recommended test. A score is not market validation.
