# Example: Turning expertise into a digital product

This is a fictional example illustrating the workflow.

## Creator profile

A freelance designer with eight years of experience
helping small businesses improve their brand identity.

## Audience hypothesis

Early-stage service businesses that struggle to create
a consistent professional brand.

## Problem hypothesis

Owners waste time creating inconsistent marketing materials
and struggle to communicate a recognizable identity.

## Expertise

The creator has repeatable experience in:
- Brand positioning.
- Visual identity.
- Design systems.
- Practical brand guidelines.

## UVZ hypothesis

Help early-stage service businesses build a coherent,
usable brand identity without needing a full agency engagement.

## Product concept

A guided brand identity toolkit and implementation programme.

## Product charter outline

1. Define brand positioning.
2. Identify customer and differentiation.
3. Establish visual direction.
4. Build a reusable identity system.
5. Create practical marketing templates.
6. Implement and review consistency.

## Validation experiment

Interview ten business owners and offer a paid pilot
to a small number of qualified participants.

## Important limitation

This example is a hypothesis, not evidence of actual
customer demand or commercial success.
