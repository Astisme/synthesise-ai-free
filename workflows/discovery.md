# Discovery Workflow

## Objective
Build an evidence-based picture of the creator's capabilities.

## Steps

1. Understand the user's goal.
2. Explore their professional background.
3. Identify specialist knowledge.
4. Identify repeatable achievements.
5. Explore unusual experiences.
6. Identify preferred audiences.
7. Establish constraints and resources.
8. Summarize expertise assets.

## Questions

Ask conversationally:
- What do people already come to you for?
- What have you helped people achieve?
- What do you know from experience that others struggle with?
- Which problems do you enjoy solving?
- What evidence supports your expertise?

## Output

An expertise inventory, credibility assessment,
potential audiences and remaining questions.
