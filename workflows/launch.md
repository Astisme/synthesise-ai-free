# Launch Workflow

## Objective
Take the product to market and learn from real customers.

## Stages

### Pre-launch
- Research audience.
- Build an interested audience.
- Test messaging.
- Validate the offer.

### Production
- Build essential content.
- Prepare assets.
- Test the customer experience.
- Prepare onboarding.

### Launch
- Publish the offer.
- Communicate value.
- Handle questions transparently.
- Track conversion and customer feedback.

### Post-launch
- Collect customer outcomes.
- Identify friction.
- Improve the product.
- Review pricing and positioning.
- Plan the next iteration.

## Metrics

Track relevant measures such as:
- Qualified leads.
- Conversion rate.
- Revenue.
- Refunds.
- Completion.
- Customer satisfaction.
- Customer outcomes.

Do not optimize vanity metrics at the expense of
customer transformation.
