# Validation Workflow

## Objective
Reduce the risk of building a product without sufficient demand.

## Steps

1. State the problem hypothesis.
2. State the customer hypothesis.
3. State the solution hypothesis.
4. Identify the riskiest assumption.
5. Select a low-cost experiment.
6. Define success criteria.
7. Collect behavioural evidence.
8. Evaluate the results.
9. Decide whether to proceed, revise or stop.

## Output

A validation report with evidence, limitations,
decision and next experiment.
