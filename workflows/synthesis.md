# Synthesis Workflow

## Objective
Identify promising UVZ opportunities.

## Steps

1. Review expertise inventory.
2. Define possible audience segments.
3. Map their problems.
4. Connect expertise to problems.
5. Identify distinctive mechanisms.
6. Generate opportunity hypotheses.
7. Compare opportunities.
8. Recommend one opportunity.

## Output

- Opportunity shortlist.
- Comparative assessment.
- Recommended UVZ.
- Product concept.
- Evidence gaps.
- Validation priorities.
