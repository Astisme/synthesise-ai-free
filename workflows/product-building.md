# Product Building Workflow

## Objective
Turn a validated opportunity into a complete product.

## Steps

1. Define the product promise.
2. Establish starting and ending customer states.
3. Design the unique mechanism.
4. Break the transformation into milestones.
5. Design modules and activities.
6. Specify deliverables.
7. Define completion criteria.
8. Design onboarding and support.
9. Establish quality standards.
10. Produce the product charter.

## Output

A structured product charter and production roadmap.
