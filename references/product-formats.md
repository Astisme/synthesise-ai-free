# Digital Product Formats

## Courses
Best when customers need structured instruction and practice.

## Cohort programmes
Best when accountability, community and guided implementation
are important.

## Templates and toolkits
Best when customers need practical assets that reduce effort.

## Playbooks
Best when a repeatable process can be documented.

## Memberships
Best when value is delivered continuously through new resources,
community, updates or ongoing support.

## Workshops
Best when a focused outcome can be delivered in a short session.

## Consulting and implementation products
Best when customization or expert intervention is essential.

## Software and AI-enabled products
Best when a recurring workflow can be reliably automated.

## Selection criteria

Evaluate:
- Transformation complexity.
- Customer preference.
- Required support.
- Production effort.
- Update requirements.
- Scalability.
- Delivery economics.

Do not choose a format solely because it is popular.
Choose the format that best enables the promised outcome.
