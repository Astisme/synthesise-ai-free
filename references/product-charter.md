# Product Charter Framework

## 1. Executive summary
Describe the product, audience and intended transformation.

## 2. Positioning
- Product name.
- Category.
- One-sentence description.
- Differentiation.

## 3. Ideal customer
- Profile.
- Current situation.
- Motivations.
- Constraints.
- Readiness.

## 4. Core problem
Describe the problem, its causes and consequences.

## 5. Desired transformation
Define the starting state and desired ending state.

## 6. Product promise
State a specific, realistic outcome within a defined scope.

## 7. Unique mechanism
Explain the process that makes the transformation possible.

## 8. Product architecture
For each module specify:
- Module name.
- Objective.
- Concepts.
- Activities.
- Deliverables.
- Completion criteria.
- Contribution to the overall transformation.

## 9. Customer journey
Map discovery, purchase, onboarding, implementation,
completion and ongoing use.

## 10. Deliverables
List tangible resources, templates, lessons, tools,
workbooks, sessions or other assets.

## 11. Support model
Define support channels, boundaries and service levels.

## 12. Success metrics
Identify customer outcome metrics and product metrics.

## 13. Risks and assumptions
Document dependencies, exclusions and unvalidated claims.

## 14. Validation plan
Specify the next experiments and decision criteria.

## 15. Commercial model
Define format, pricing hypotheses, tiers and delivery costs.

## 16. Next actions
Provide an ordered implementation roadmap.
