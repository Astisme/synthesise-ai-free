# Monetization Strategy

## Offer architecture

Define:
- Core product.
- Primary transformation.
- Delivery format.
- Price hypothesis.
- Support boundaries.
- Optional enhancements.
- Complementary offers.

## Pricing

Consider:
- Customer value.
- Alternative solutions.
- Audience purchasing power.
- Delivery costs.
- Support requirements.
- Positioning.
- Competitive context.

Treat proposed prices as hypotheses until tested.

## Revenue models

- One-time purchase.
- Payment plans.
- Subscription.
- Cohort enrollment.
- Licensing.
- Services attached to products.
- Tiered access.

Recurring revenue is appropriate only when recurring value
is actually delivered.

## Offer clarity

A customer should understand:
1. Who the product is for.
2. What problem it solves.
3. What outcome it supports.
4. How it works.
5. What is included.
6. What it costs.
7. What is not included.

## Ethical marketing

Avoid:
- Guaranteed financial outcomes.
- False scarcity.
- Fabricated testimonials.
- Unsupported income claims.
- Misleading before-and-after promises.

Sell a credible transformation, not an unrealistic fantasy.
