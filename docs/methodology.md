# Product development methodology

Synthesise AI Free treats product development as a sequence of risk-reducing decisions, not a prompt chain.

1. Creator-market fit discovery: inventory capability, proof, repeatable outcomes, audience access, motivation and constraints.
2. UVZ synthesis: generate multiple overlaps between creator advantage, customer problem and distinctive mechanism; rank transparently and show uncertainty.
3. Market intelligence: map competitors, alternatives, DIY and status quo; research customer language and category dynamics using dated evidence.
4. Validation: identify the riskiest assumption and run the smallest meaningful experiment with predeclared thresholds.
5. Product architecture: design backward from a realistic transformation; map outcomes to milestones, curriculum, activities and artifacts.
6. Commercial design: develop positioning, tiers, pricing hypotheses and basic unit economics.
7. Launch and iteration: ship the minimum sellable experience, measure the customer journey and learn from outcomes.

At each gate choose proceed, iterate, pivot or stop. Record why, what evidence changed the decision and what uncertainty remains. Scores prioritize inquiry; they do not replace customer evidence.
